## Description
Why do Detroit Red Wings fans throw an OCTOPUS on the ice? 🐙 And why is there one hidden in every ZAM episode? The gang finally explains – and Biscuit is not happy about it.

🐟 On April 15, 1952, during the playoffs in Detroit, two brothers who ran a fish shop threw an octopus onto the ice. Legend says the eight arms stood for the eight playoff wins it took to win the Stanley Cup back then – and Detroit won all eight games that spring.
Sources: https://vault.si.com/vault/1994/05/16/a-tradition-with-legs-alas-this-spring-the-red-wings-house-specialty-became-shark-bait · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legend_of_the_Octopus

🧊 Later, Red Wings ice machine driver Al Sobotka became famous for twirling the octopuses over his head.
Sources: https://www.npr.org/2008/05/12/90371601/detroit-red-wings-good-luck-charm-the-octopus · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Sobotka

🚫 In April 2008 the NHL banned twirling on the ice, with a $10,000 fine. A few weeks later (May 2008) it was allowed again – but only at the ice machine gate.
Sources: https://vault.si.com/vault/2008/06/02/seafood-for-thought · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Sobotka
