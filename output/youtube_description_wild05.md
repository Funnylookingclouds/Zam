## Description
3 goals in 21 seconds, a goalie who shot and scored, and a game that ended at 2:25 in the morning 🤯 Three of the wildest moments in NHL history – acted out by the Hockey Life gang.

⚡ Fastest hat trick ever: On March 23, 1952, Chicago's Bill Mosienko scored three goals in just 21 seconds against the New York Rangers (Chicago won 7–6). More than 70 years later, it's still the fastest hat trick in NHL history.
Source: https://www.nhl.com/news/bill-mosienko-scores-fastest-hat-trick-in-nhl-history-290802314 · https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/fastest-nhl-hat-trick

🥅 First goalie to shoot and score: On December 8, 1987, Flyers goalie Ron Hextall fired the puck the length of the ice into an empty net in a 5–2 win over the Boston Bruins – the first NHL goalie to score by shooting the puck into the net.
Source: https://www.nhl.com/news/this-date-in-nhl-history-december-8-284340322

😴 The longest game ever: On March 24, 1936, the Detroit Red Wings and Montreal Maroons played six overtimes in a playoff semifinal. Mud Bruneteau finally scored after 116:30 of extra time – 176:30 in total – at about 2:25 a.m. Detroit won 1–0.
Source: https://www.nhl.com/news/this-date-in-nhl-history-march-24-279805016 · https://www.nhl.com/news/detroit-red-wings-win-longest-overtime-playoff-game-in-nhl-history-289219150
