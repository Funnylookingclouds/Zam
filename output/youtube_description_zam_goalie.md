## Description
An ice machine driver played goalie in the NHL – and WON. 🤯 Zam has never been more proud. Biscuit has never been more upset.

🥅 On February 22, 2020, the Carolina Hurricanes lost BOTH of their goalies during a game in Toronto. In came their 42-year-old emergency backup goalie, David Ayres – who drove the ice machine at the team's practice rink. He made 8 saves, Carolina won 6–3, and he became the first emergency backup goalie ever to win an NHL game.
