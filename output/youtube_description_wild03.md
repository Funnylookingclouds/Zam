## Description
Bobby Orr's flying goal, Gretzky's 50 goals in 39 games, Detroit's legendary octopus and Teemu Selänne shooting his own glove – hockey's wildest NHL moments in under a minute! 🏒🤯

🦅 **Bobby Orr – the flying goal (May 10, 1970):** 40 seconds into overtime of Game 4 of the Cup Final, Orr slid the puck past Glenn Hall and was sent flying by Noel Picard's trip – Boston's first Stanley Cup since 1941.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/bruins-blues-1970-stanley-cup-final-more-than-orr-goal-307525846
ESPN: https://www.espn.com/blog/statsinfo/post/_/id/131869/on-this-date-bobby-orrs-goal-wins-a-stanley-cup

5️⃣0️⃣ **Wayne Gretzky – 50 in 39 (Dec 30, 1981):** five goals in Edmonton's 7–5 win over Philadelphia, the 50th into an empty net with three seconds left.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/this-date-in-nhl-history-december-30-285055510
Wikipedia: https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/50_goals_in_50_games

🐙 **The Detroit octopus (April 1952):** fish sellers Pete and Jerry Cusimano threw an octopus on the ice – eight arms for the eight wins it then took to win the Stanley Cup.
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legend_of_the_Octopus
ClickOnDetroit: https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/local/2025/04/15/how-an-octopus-became-the-mascot-of-the-detroit-red-wings

🧤 **Teemu Selänne – the glove shot (Mar 2, 1993):** his 54th goal broke Mike Bossy's rookie record; he tossed his glove in the air and "shot" it down with his stick. He finished with 76 goals – still the NHL rookie record.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/teemu-selanne-made-history-24-years-ago-with-jets-287218136
Guinness World Records: https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/most-goals-scored-by-a-rookie-in-an-nhl-season
