## Description
Pucky got the Stanley Cup for ONE day. What could go wrong? Historically… everything. 😂🏆 Three true Stanley Cup disasters, retold by the ZAM gang.

🚗 1924: After winning the Cup, Montreal Canadiens players left it on the side of the road on their way to a party – and found it later right where they'd left it, in the snow.
🏊 1991: After the Penguins' first title, Mario Lemieux tested whether the Cup could float in his swimming pool. It sank.
🔤 The Cup has typos: the Hockey Hall of Fame lists "BQSTQN BRUINS" (1971-72) and "TORONTO MAPLE LEAES" (1962-63) among its engraving errors.
