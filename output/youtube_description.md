## Description
Mario Lemieux's 5 goals 5 ways, a 67-penalty-minute night, goalies fighting AND scoring – hockey's wildest NHL moments of all time in under a minute! 🏒🤯

🥅 **Mario Lemieux – 5 goals, 5 ways (Dec 31, 1988):** In Pittsburgh's 8–6 win over New Jersey, Lemieux scored at even strength, on the power play, shorthanded, on a penalty shot and into an empty net – the only time it has happened in NHL history. In 2017 it was voted the greatest moment in NHL history.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/mario-lemieux-five-goal-game-is-greatest-nhl-moment-294046120
CBS Sports: https://www.cbssports.com/nhl/news/random-flashback-the-time-mario-lemieux-scored-five-goals-five-different-ways

⏱️ **Randy Holt – 67 penalty minutes (Mar 11, 1979):** Playing for the Los Angeles Kings against the Philadelphia Flyers, Holt racked up 67 PIM in one game – all in the first period. It's still the NHL single-game record.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/kings/news/randy-holt-s-record-setting-night-316264320
Guinness World Records: https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/113761-most-penalty-minutes-in-an-nhl-game

🥊 **Fight Night at the Joe (Mar 26, 1997):** Detroit vs Colorado turned into one of hockey's most famous brawls – even goalies Patrick Roy and Mike Vernon dropped their masks and gloves at centre ice.
ClickOnDetroit: https://clickondetroit.com/sports/2021/03/26/the-history-behind-iconic-red-wings-avalanche-brawl-of-march-26-1997

🎯 **Linus Ullmark's goalie goal (Feb 25, 2023):** With Vancouver's net empty, the Bruins goalie fired the puck the length of the rink and scored in a 3–1 win – the first goalie goal in the NHL since Pekka Rinne in 2020.
NHL.com: https://nhl.com/news/bruins-linus-ullmark-goal-dream-come-true-341525682
CBC: https://www.cbc.ca/1.6760688
