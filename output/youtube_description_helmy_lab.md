## Description
The NHL's craziest numbers: a 21-second hat trick, Gretzky's 1,963 assists (more than anyone's total points), Ovechkin's record 895th goal and how much the Stanley Cup weighs – Helmy's number lab in under a minute! 🔢🏒

⏱️ **21 seconds:** on March 23, 1952, Chicago's Bill Mosienko scored three goals in 21 seconds against the Rangers – still the fastest hat trick in NHL history.
NHL.com: https://nhl.com/news/bill-mosienko-scores-fastest-hat-trick-in-nhl-history-290802314
Guinness World Records: https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/fastest-nhl-hat-trick

🎯 **1,963:** Wayne Gretzky's regular-season assists – more than any other player's career total points (Jaromír Jágr is second all-time with 1,921 points).
ESPN: https://global.espn.com/nhl/story/_/id/41679163/which-player-most-career-points-nhl-history
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_career_achievements_by_Wayne_Gretzky

👑 **895:** on April 6, 2025, Alex Ovechkin scored his 895th goal, passing Gretzky's 894 to become the NHL's all-time goals leader.
NHL.com: https://nhl.com/news/alex-ovechkin-passes-wayne-gretzky-for-nhl-goals-record
CBC: https://amp.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.7503198
