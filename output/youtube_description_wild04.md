## Description
The longest game in NHL history (six overtimes, over at 2:25 a.m.), an own goal on a birthday, Gordie Howe playing at 51 and the fastest goal ever – hockey's wildest NHL moments in under a minute! 🏒🤯

🌙 **The longest game (March 24–25, 1936):** Detroit beat the Montreal Maroons 1–0 in the sixth overtime – 176:30 of hockey – when Mud Bruneteau scored at 2:25 a.m.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/detroit-red-wings-win-longest-overtime-playoff-game-in-nhl-history-289219150
Hockey-Reference: https://www.hockey-reference.com/friv/longest-games.cgi

🎂 **Steve Smith's own goal (April 30, 1986):** in Game 7 vs Calgary, Edmonton's Steve Smith tried an outlet pass from behind his net; it banked in off goalie Grant Fuhr's skate for a 3–2 Flames lead that held – on Smith's 23rd birthday.
SI: https://www.si.com/nhl/2016/05/02/edmonton-oilers-steve-smith-own-goal-1986-nhl-playoffs
FlamesNation: https://flamesnation.ca/news/on-this-day-in-1986-steve-smith-scored-on-his-own-net

👴 **Gordie Howe at 51 (1979–80):** Mr. Hockey played all 80 games for Hartford – and on March 9, 1980 shared a shift with his sons Mark and Marty.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/gordie-howe-skates-on-same-line-as-sons-287467494
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/gordie-howe-retired-june-4-1980-remained-face-of-hockey-289754646

⚡ **Fastest goal ever:** the NHL record for a goal from the opening faceoff is 5 seconds – shared by four players.
