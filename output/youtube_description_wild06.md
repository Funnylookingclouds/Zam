## Description
5 goals 5 different ways, 10 points in one game, and the night someone threw an octopus on the ice 🐙 Three of the wildest moments in NHL history – with a very unimpressed goalie.

🏒 Five goals, five ways: On December 31, 1988, Mario Lemieux scored five goals in the Penguins' 8–6 win over the New Jersey Devils – at even strength, on the power play, shorthanded, on a penalty shot and into an empty net. No one else has ever done it, and fans voted it the greatest moment in NHL history.
Source: https://nhl.com/news/ken-daneyko-remembers-mario-lemieux-scoring-five-ways-292265468 · https://www.nhl.com/news/mario-lemieux-five-goal-game-is-greatest-nhl-moment-294046120

🔟 Ten points in one game: On February 7, 1976, Toronto's Darryl Sittler had 10 points – six goals and four assists – in an 11–4 win over the Boston Bruins. It's still the NHL single-game record.
Source: https://www.nhl.com/news/darryl-sittler-10-point-game-278406152

🐙 The first octopus: On April 15, 1952, Detroit fans Pete and Jerry Cusimano threw an octopus onto the ice – eight legs for the eight playoff wins it took to win the Stanley Cup back then. Detroit swept to the Cup that spring, and the tradition lives on.
Source: https://nhl.com/news/this-date-in-nhl-history-april-15-280304050
