Agenty's Prospect Watch: the young NHL talents everyone is talking about – Gavin McKenna, Ivar Stenberg, Matthew Schaefer and Macklin Celebrini. 🏒⭐

1️⃣ **Gavin McKenna:** Toronto won the 2026 draft lottery with 8.5% odds and took the Penn State winger first overall (51 points in 35 games as a freshman).
Yahoo Sports: https://sports.yahoo.com/nhl/breaking-news/article/nhl-draft-2026-maple-leafs-select-penn-states-gavin-mckenna-with-no-1-overall-pick-231901164.html

Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivar_Stenberg
Daily Faceoff: https://www.dailyfaceoff.com/news/ivar-stenberg-2026-nhl-draft-profile-scouting-report

NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/matthew-schaefer-wins-calder-trophy-for-2025-26-season
ESPN: https://www.espn.com/nhl/story/_/id/48760989/islanders-matthew-schaefer-unanimous-calder-trophy-winner
