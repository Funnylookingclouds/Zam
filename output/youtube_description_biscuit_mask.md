## Description
NHL goalies used to play with NO masks?! 😳 Here's the true story of Jacques Plante and the night the goalie mask changed hockey forever – told by Biscuit, who has opinions.

🥅 On November 1, 1959, at Madison Square Garden, a shot from Rangers star Andy Bathgate hit Montreal Canadiens goalie Jacques Plante in the face. He needed seven stitches. Montreal had no backup goalie, so Plante refused to go back in without the mask he'd been wearing in practice. Coach Toe Blake hated the idea – but agreed. Montreal won 3–1, and the masked Plante went 10-0-1 that November.
Sources: https://www.nhl.com/news/jacques-plante-becomes-first-goalie-to-wear-mask-290431748 · https://www.nhl.com/news/andy-bathgate-jacques-plante-goalie-mask-279175384 · https://www.espn.com/classic/s/add_plante_jacques.html

🚫 On March 8, 1960, Blake asked Plante to play one game without the mask, in Detroit. Montreal lost 3–0 – and the mask came back the very next night.
Sources: https://www.nhl.com/news/jacques-plante-becomes-first-goalie-to-wear-mask-290431748 · https://amp.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.4886259

🤓 Helmy's footnote: Plante wasn't the very first. Clint Benedict briefly wore a leather mask in 1930, but Plante is the one who made the mask stick.
Source: https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/goaltender-masks
