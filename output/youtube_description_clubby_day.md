## Description
Old-school hockey facts that sound fake but are 100% true: the first indoor game in 1875, goalies banned from falling down, no forward passes, the accidental curved stick and the last player without a helmet. Story time with Clubby! 👴🏒

🏟️ **The first indoor game (Mar 3, 1875):** played at the Victoria Skating Rink in Montreal – McGill beat Victoria 2–1, nine players a side.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/first-indoor-hockey-game-played-142-years-ago-287304824
McGill: https://200.mcgill.ca/history/writing-the-rules-of-hockey/

🥅 **No falling, goalies!** Until Jan 9, 1918, NHL goalies were penalized for dropping to the ice. Clint Benedict ("Praying Benny") kept "accidentally" falling until the rule was scrapped.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/clint-benedict-was-one-of-nhl-s-first-great-goalies-286889904

➡️ **No forward passes:** forward passing in the attacking zone wasn't allowed until the 1929–30 season – and scoring more than doubled.
The Hockey News: https://thehockeynews.com/news/article/rules-that-changed-the-game
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1929%E2%80%9330_NHL_season

🏒 **The accidental curve:** in the 1960s, Chicago's Stan Mikita bent his blade by accident – and the curved stick took off.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/sticking-to-the-story-312628480

⛑️ **Helmets:** the NHL required helmets for new players from 1979; Craig MacTavish, grandfathered in, played without one until he retired in 1997 – the last to do so.
The Hockey News: https://thehockeynews.com/news/all-access/helmet-holdouts-the-last-players-to-wear-helmets-in-the-nhl
