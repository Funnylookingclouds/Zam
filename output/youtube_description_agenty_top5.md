## Description
Meet Agenty, the ZAM gang's new (very confident) agent – and his top 5 hockey prospects of all time: Lindros, McDavid, Crosby, Lemieux and Gretzky. 🏒🤯

5️⃣ **Eric Lindros:** drafted first overall by Quebec in 1991 – and refused to sign. In 1992 Philadelphia paid six players (including the rights to Peter Forsberg), two first-round picks and $15 million.
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Lindros_trade
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/eric-lindros-was-subject-of-controversy-at-1991-nhl-draft-281006206

4️⃣ **Connor McDavid:** granted exceptional player status in 2012, letting him enter the OHL draft at 15 – a year early.
The Hockey News: https://thehockeynews.com/news/news/connor-mcdavid-granted-exceptional-player-status-becomes-eligible-for-ohl-draft-in-april

3️⃣ **Sidney Crosby:** the weighted 2005 draft lottery after the lockout became known as the "Sidney Crosby Sweepstakes" – Pittsburgh won it.
Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2005_NHL_entry_draft
NHL.com: https://nhl.com/news/this-date-in-nhl-history-july-30-281225466

2️⃣ **Mario Lemieux:** 133 goals and 282 points in 70 games for Laval in 1983–84 – still QMJHL records.
CHL: https://chl.ca/lhjmq/en/article/40-years-ago-marios-magic/

1️⃣ **Wayne Gretzky:** 378 goals in a single minor-hockey season at age 10 in Brantford.
