# YouTube channel description (ZAM · @ZamHockey)

Hockey in under a minute! 🏒⚡

ZAM is a cartoon hockey channel hosted by a gang of hockey-gear characters: Zam the ice machine, Pucky the puck, Biscuit the walrus goalie, Helmy the stats-nerd helmet, Clubby the old-school stick and Agenty the prospect hunter.

🏒 DAILY FACTS – the NHL's wildest moments, records and history, acted out by the gang (every fact checked, sources in each description).
🎬 SPECIAL EDITIONS – funny cartoon episodes: a day at work with Pucky, Biscuit's worst day, summer camp and more.

New Shorts every week. Subscribe so you don't miss the next one! 🔔

All plays are animated recreations – no broadcast footage. Not affiliated with the NHL or any team.
