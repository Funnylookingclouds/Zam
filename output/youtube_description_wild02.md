## Description
Ovechkin's "The Goal", Darryl Sittler's 10-point night, the first lacrosse-style goal in NHL history and Florida's legendary "rat trick" – hockey's wildest NHL moments in under a minute! 🏒🤯

🥅 **Alex Ovechkin – "The Goal" (Jan 16, 2006):** In Washington's 6–1 win at Phoenix, rookie Ovechkin got tangled up, fell and – sliding on his back – hooked the puck one-handed past Brian Boucher. Wayne Gretzky was coaching the Coyotes.
NHL.com: https://nhl.com/news/this-date-in-nhl-history-january-16-285592536
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/alex-ovechkin-still-unsure-how-he-scored-the-goal-285608274

🔟 **Darryl Sittler – 10 points (Feb 7, 1976):** Six goals and four assists in Toronto's 11–4 win over Boston. Still the NHL single-game record.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/news/darryl-sittler-10-point-game-278406152

🥍 **Andrei Svechnikov – first lacrosse-style goal (Oct 29, 2019):** Carolina's Svechnikov scooped the puck onto his blade behind the net and tucked it in against Calgary – the first "Michigan" goal in NHL history.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/hurricanes/news/top-2019-20-season-moments-andrei-svechnikov-lacrosse-goals-317460374
CBS Sports: https://www.cbssports.com/nhl/news/hurricanes-winger-andrei-svechnikov-pulls-off-lacrosse-style-goal-for-second-time

🐀 **The "Rat Trick" (Oct 8, 1995):** Before Florida's home opener, Scott Mellanby swatted a rat in the locker room – then scored twice with the same stick in a 4–3 win over Calgary. Fans have been throwing rubber rats on the ice ever since.
NHL.com: https://www.nhl.com/panthers/news/tales-from-the-press-box-looking-back-at-the-rat-craze-302652262
CBS News Miami: https://www.cbsnews.com/miami/news/why-panthers-fan-throw-fake-rats-on-ice-history
