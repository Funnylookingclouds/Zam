## Description
Everyone sees the puck on TV. Nobody sees the grind. 😂 Follow Pucky through a full day at work – from the 6 a.m. freezer to getting slapped at 108.8 mph and tucked back into the freezer at night.

❄️ Game pucks really are kept frozen before and during NHL games – a frozen puck doesn't bounce like a warm one, and the home team is responsible for keeping them cold.
💜 Since 2019, NHL game pucks have had a colour-changing coating that shows purple when the puck is frozen – so officials can see when it's ready.
💥 The hardest shot ever recorded at an NHL All-Star Skills Competition is 108.8 mph – Zdeno Chara, 2012.
