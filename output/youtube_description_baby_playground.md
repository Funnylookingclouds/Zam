A sunny playground song for little hockey fans 🛝 The ZAM Baby Gang go outside to play – swings, sandbox, slide and seesaw – and sing "Up the ladder, down the slide" together!

Baby Zam runs out the gate, Baby Pucky (pacifier and all) goes swing, swing, wheee, Baby Biscuit bakes a sand cake, Baby Helmy climbs up and slides down, and Baby Clubby and Baby Agenty ride the seesaw – up, down, again!
